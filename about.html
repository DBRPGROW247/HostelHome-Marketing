Modern Hostel & PG Infrastructure

Making hostel life feel a little more like home.

HostelHome is built to bring clarity and effortless control to hostel and PG operations. We eliminate physical registers, manual fee tracking, and scattered messages by connecting wardens and students through a unified desktop dashboard and mobile app.

What Do We Work For?

Managing student housing involves dozens of daily operational tasks: admitting new residents, verifying ID records, collecting monthly fees, tracking vacant beds, updating meal menus, and resolving maintenance issues.

We work to give wardens complete administrative clarity and automated financial management, while providing students with a seamless, modern mobile app experience. We believe technology should handle repetitive paperwork so management can focus on resident safety and students can focus on their future.

The Problems We Solve

Replacing manual bottlenecks with structured digital clarity.

✕

Traditional Manual Management

Prone to errors, missing records, and fee disputes

  • ✕ Paper Admission Forms: Manually collecting physical forms, photo copies, and filling physical registers for every resident.
  • ✕ Fee Reconciliation Disputes: Cross-checking bank SMS alerts, paper receipts, and cash diaries to figure out pending balances.
  • ✕ Scattered Information: Difficult to quickly locate emergency parent contacts, room allotments, or student verification details.
  • ✕ Unknown Occupancy Trends: Lack of visual insights into room occupancy, vacant spaces, and seasonal student movement.
  • ✕ Unrecorded Maintenance Complaints: Plumbing and electrical issues told verbally or over WhatsApp get lost or delayed.
✓

The HostelHome Solution

Automated, transparent, and completely organized

  • ✓ 1-Scan QR Onboarding: New students scan the hostel QR code, fill their verified profile, and join their room in 30 seconds.
  • ✓ PhonePe PG Instant Payments: Automated real-time payment verification with digital receipts (plus 1-tap manual verification).
  • ✓ Instant Digital ID Cards: Automated digital student ID cards and complete verified profiles accessible anytime.
  • ✓ Live Statistical Graphs: Visual trends of student numbers over time, total capacity, occupied beds, and vacant spaces.
  • ✓ Tracked Maintenance Desk: In-app complaint lodging with photo attachments and live status resolution tracking.
