How to get a Merchant UPI ID

Follow these simple steps to create a free merchant UPI ID for your hostel.

1

Choose a Merchant App

Download any popular UPI merchant app on your smartphone from the Play Store or App Store.

Google Pay for Business PhonePe Business Paytm for Business BharatPe
2

Register Your Business

Open the app and register using your mobile number (preferably the one linked to your hostel's bank account). Enter your business details such as "Hostel Name" and select the relevant business category (e.g., Real Estate / Rent).

3

Link Bank Account

Add the bank account where you want to receive payments from your residents. The app will verify your account via an SMS.

4

Complete KYC

Provide your PAN card or Aadhaar details to verify your identity and activate your merchant account.

5

Find Your Merchant UPI ID

Once approved, go to the Profile or QR Code section of the app. Look for your Merchant UPI ID (it usually ends with something like @okbizaxis, @ybl, or @paytm).

Next Steps

Copy the Merchant UPI ID from the app and paste it into the Merchant UPI ID field in your HostelHome settings to start receiving payments instantly.
