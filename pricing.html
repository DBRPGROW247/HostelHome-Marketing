Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans and capacity.

Are all features included in the 50-student Starter pack?

Yes! There is zero feature difference between the 50-student pack and the 120-student pack. Both packs include the entire HostelHome management suite (QR onboarding, PhonePe PG fees, digital ID cards, mess planner, occupancy graphs, and financials). The only distinction is your student capacity limit.

What if my hostel has more than 120 students?

For hostels, PG chains, or campuses with more than 120 students, choose the Custom plan and contact us. We will set up a custom capacity tailored to your exact needs.

Can I upgrade from 50 to 120 students later?

Yes. If your hostel grows, you can seamlessly upgrade your capacity at any time without losing any student records or payment history.
